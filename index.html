PALAWAN, PHILIPPINES

El Nido, One of a kind

Tucked at the northern tip of Palawan, El Nido is the gateway to the Bacuit Archipelago — a scattering of limestone islands, hidden lagoons, and powder-white beaches that has made it one of the most celebrated destinations in the Philippines and Southeast Asia. El Nido's been ready for you. Have you been ready for it?

Activities & Things to Do

Ways to spend your days in El Nido

Island Hopping

Boat between the islands and hidden coves of the Bacuit Archipelago.

Snorkeling

See coral gardens and reef fish in the shallow, clear water.

Swimming

Cool off in lagoons enclosed by limestone cliffs.

Kayaking

Paddle into narrow lagoons that boats can't reach.

Scuba Diving

Explore reef walls and wrecks around the archipelago.

Cliff Jumping

Jump from limestone ledges into deep lagoon pools.

Beach Activities

Relax or play beach volleyball at Nacpan or Las Cabanas.

Hiking

Hike up Taraw Cliff for a view over the islands.

Sunset Watching

Watch the sky change color over Bacuit Bay.

Caves & Lagoons

Explore Secret Lagoon and Cathedral Cave.
