About El Nido

The Nest of the Philippines - where limestone meets the sea

Location & Geography

Where is El Nido?

El Nido sits at the northernmost tip of Palawan Island in the Philippines, facing the South China Sea to the west and the Sulu Sea to the east. It serves as the main gateway to the Bacuit Archipelago — a sweeping cluster of 45 islands, islets, and coral reefs. The town is accessible by direct flight from Manila and Cebu via El Nido Airport (Lio Airport).

Brief History

From fishing village to world-class destination

El Nido — Spanish for "The Nest" — takes its name from the edible nests of swiftlets built inside its towering limestone cliffs. Once a quiet fishing village known only to locals, it gained international recognition in the 1980s for its diving, lagoons, and dramatic seascapes. Today it ranks among the most sought-after travel destinations in the world.
